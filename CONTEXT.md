# Route to Success

The public website of Route to Success, a student club at BASIS Chandler. The site exists to get families to register for the club's Math Competition, to attract Sponsors, and to show the other Events the club runs or takes part in.

## Language

### The club

**Route to Success**:
The student club at BASIS Chandler that runs the Math Competition and the other Events on this site.
_Avoid_: RTS, the org

**BASIS Chandler**:
The school the club belongs to. It is the "BC" in the domain name.
_Avoid_: BC (in prose), the school

**Club Adviser**:
The BASIS Chandler teacher responsible for the club, who approves what the site says about the school.
_Avoid_: sponsor (for the teacher), advisor

**Volunteer**:
A person who helps the club run an Event without being a Competitor.

**Sponsor**:
A business or person who gives money or goods to support the club, usually in return for recognition.
_Avoid_: donor (when recognition is involved), partner

**Sponsorship Prospectus**:
The detailed document emailed to prospective Sponsors, describing what they give and what recognition they receive.
_Avoid_: sponsor packet, deck

### Events

**Event**:
Something the club runs, or takes part in, on a specific date.
_Avoid_: program, activity

**Program**:
Something the club does on an ongoing basis, not tied to a single date.

**Math Competition**:
The club's yearly flagship Event: a dated contest where Competitors solve math problems across Rounds, grouped into Divisions. Each year's occurrence is its own Math Competition (e.g. "the 2026 Math Competition").
_Avoid_: math comp, comp, contest

**Stage**:
Where a Math Competition is in its lifecycle: Upcoming, Registration Open, Registration Closed, or Past.
_Avoid_: status, phase

**Beneficiary**:
A nonprofit that a Math Competition raises money for, separate from the club itself.
_Avoid_: charity, cause

**In-kind Donation**:
Goods bought with money the school collected, then given to a Beneficiary instead of cash.
_Avoid_: gift, supplies drive

**Theme**:
The visual identity (colour and artwork) of one Math Competition, layered on top of the club's own look.
_Avoid_: branding, skin

**Asian Night Market**:
A BASIS Chandler school Event that the club takes part in, but does not run.
_Avoid_: ANM

### The Math Competition

**Round**:
One timed section of a Math Competition: Sprint, Target, Team, or Countdown.

**Division**:
A grade band that groups Competitors so they compete against students of similar age (Division 1, 2, 3).
_Avoid_: Dv1, level, bracket

**Competitor**:
A student entered in a Math Competition.
_Avoid_: participant, contestant, kid

**Parent**:
The adult who registers a Competitor and pays the Entry Fee.
_Avoid_: guardian, customer, user

**Registration**:
A Parent's submission that enters one Competitor into one Division of one Math Competition.
_Avoid_: sign-up, enrollment

**Walk-in**:
A Competitor registered in person on the day of a Math Competition, after online Registration has closed. Each Math Competition decides whether it accepts Walk-ins.
_Avoid_: day-of sign-up, late registration

**Entry Fee**:
The amount paid per Registration. It is set separately for each Math Competition.
_Avoid_: registration fee, cost

**Payment Method**:
How Parents pay the Entry Fee for one Math Competition: either School Payment (through BASIS Chandler, which passes the money on to the Beneficiary) or Direct Donation (straight to the Beneficiary, with proof attached to the Registration). Each Math Competition picks one.
_Avoid_: checkout, payment option

**Photo Release**:
A Parent's consent for images of their Competitor to be published. Only people covered by a Photo Release may appear in photos on the site.

## Relationships

- A **Math Competition** has several **Rounds** and several **Divisions**.
- A **Parent** makes a **Registration** for each **Competitor**; each **Registration** places that **Competitor** in exactly one **Division**.
- Each **Math Competition** sets its own **Entry Fee**, and is in exactly one **Stage** at a time.
- A **Math Competition** may have a **Beneficiary**, and has its own **Theme** and its own **Sponsors**.
- Each **Math Competition** has exactly one **Payment Method**. An **In-kind Donation** only happens with School Payment.
- Rounds and Divisions have stayed the same across Math Competitions (2024, 2025, 2026).
- Money raised through the club is received by BASIS Chandler's nonprofit, not by the club itself.
- Demographics about **Competitors** are published only as totals, never in a form that could identify a child.
